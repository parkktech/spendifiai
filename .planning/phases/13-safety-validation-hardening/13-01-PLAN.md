  <files>tests/Unit/BannedPhraseSystemPromptsTest.php</files>
  <action>
Create a static source-scan Pest test mirroring the EstimatedValueGuardTest pattern (read app/Services/*.php with file(), line-by-line scan). Per binding constraint 1, enforcement scopes to the THREE v2.1 optimizer Claude call sites only: app/Services/NarrationService.php, app/Services/OptimizationReportNarratorService.php, app/Services/InterviewOrchestratorService.php. The v1.0 services (SavingsAnalyzerService, SavingsTargetPlannerService, AlternativeSuggestionService, SyncSummaryService) are NOT enforced here — they are ruled in the Task-2 worksheet and handed to the SAFE-05 report per binding constraint 1.

Reuse the banned-phrase list by requiring tests/Unit/BannedPhraseTemplatesTest.php and calling its bannedPhraseList() function — do NOT redefine the phrases in this file (single source of truth; also avoids two lists drifting).

CRITICAL false-positive rule (research Pitfall 2): these system prompts legitimately QUOTE the banned phrases inside prohibition instructions (verified: NarrationService line 55 and OptimizationReportNarratorService line 57 contain the phrases in "NEVER say ..." form, and docblock lines beginning with *). A naive str_contains scan would fail on the very files that correctly implement the ban. Therefore, before flagging a banned-phrase MATCH on a line, SKIP that match when: (a) the line is a pure comment line (ltrim starts with // or # or *), OR (b) a negation cue token — never, not, don't, "do not", avoid, ban, prohibited — appears within 3 tokens IMMEDIATELY BEFORE the matched phrase's position (scope the cue to the prohibition it governs, NOT the whole line). Do NOT skip merely because a negation cue appears somewhere on the line: a line like "You must act — do not wait" opens with an assertive phrase and MUST FAIL the gate even though a "do not" follows later. As an equally acceptable belt-and-suspenders alternative to the 3-token window, use an explicit LINE-ANCHORED allowlist of the two known prohibition lines (NarrationService:55, OptimizationReportNarratorService:57) — never a blanket whole-line negation-cue skip. On surviving matches, a case-insensitive match of any banned phrase is a violation. Collect violations as "basename:lineNumber — phrase" and assert the collection is empty with a message naming each violation. Document the narrowed skip rule and its rationale in the test file header.

Add a companion assertion that BannedPhraseTemplatesTest.php still exists and its bannedPhraseList() is callable (guards the reuse link from silently breaking).

Test-the-test (mandatory anti-vacuity step): once the gate is GREEN, prove it is RED-provable — temporarily insert an assertive banned phrase on a non-comment, non-prohibition line of one enforced service file, run `--filter=BannedPhraseSystem`, confirm it goes RED, then REVERT the mutation and confirm it returns GREEN. Also confirm the narrowed skip does not over-suppress: a temporary "assertive-phrase … later do-not" line must go RED. Record the RED→revert→GREEN demonstration (file, line, phrase, both results) in the SUMMARY. (Scripting the mutation+revert inside the verify is acceptable, but the SUMMARY evidence is required either way.)
